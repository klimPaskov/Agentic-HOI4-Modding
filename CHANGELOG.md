# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Catch a joint that snaps round in one frame (UNIT-ANIM-SPIN). Keys on opposite sides of the quaternion sphere make Blender interpolate the long way, and the baked clip swings a limb or a held polearm most of a circle in a frame or two. `check_unit_files.py` fails a clip in which a joint that skins vertices turns farther between two frames than any vanilla joint of its group (soldiers, riders and creatures 37 degrees at 30 fps, vehicles 113, planes and ships 43). Attach nodes that skin nothing are skipped. `calibrate_deformation.py` measures it (`turn_per_frame`), and `--only` updates the cheap per-clip measures in an existing calibration without the full run. Clips one mod already ships turn a shoulder 144 degrees in one frame.
- Add four live-authoring helpers and the lessons behind them to the 3D route. `normalize_quaternion_keys` flips quaternion keys that sit in the opposite hemisphere from the key before, so a prop or limb no longer turns the long way between keys. `stretch_share` reports the share of edges UNIT-ANIM-STRETCH counts at a frame, from the kit's own constants (a hand-made gauge read about 0.8 times the kit's number). `lowest_regions` lists the lowest point of each body region and prop at a frame, so a dead body is lifted at the group that props it up. `clear_sharp_faces` removes import sharp flags that push a stream past 65,535 vertices. The live-authoring guide gains short paragraphs on bone heads, cache keys, coats, wings, props, digitigrade legs, tails, cloaks, bone islands and death-clip corrections.
- Judge a held weapon as one part. UNIT-LOOSE-PART judged a polearm fitted as its own shape and bound to the hand piece by piece: a band set off the shaft "floated", and the spike, anchored to the helmet it sits beside at rest, "drifted" half the unit's height in the attack. A separate shape whose every vertex follows one bone wholly (the weapon `fit_prop.py` binds) is now one part, judged against the body vertices that follow the same bone (the hand that holds it, or the nearest ancestor bone with any): it must touch them at rest and stay on them in every frame. Other parts are judged as before, and vanilla's measurements, which set the limit, do not change.
- Make 3D review rounds converge (decision 3D-7: the bar stays at no defects). `review_unit.py` takes fixed notes for the unit (`--note`, repeatable, or `--note-file`), for example that a torn hem is the unit's intended look, and puts them in every round's request. The reviewer answers the quality bar item by item in a `CHECKLIST:` block, and a reply that skips an item is recorded as a fail (`--no-checklist` drops it). `--clips <clip> ...` makes an intermediate round that renders and judges only the changed clips and the rest pose. The round is recorded in `review_scope.json` and the verdict, and a passing intermediate round names the full round with a new reviewer that acceptance still needs.
- Write every 3D clip at 30 frames a second. io_pdx_mesh writes the Blender scene's rate, and a scene from factory settings runs at 24, so every clip one mod exported through the kit came out at 24 while vanilla soldiers, riders and horses play at 30. The adapter's animation export and `live_helpers.export_clip` now scale an action's keys so it keeps its length in seconds (24 to 30 is 1.25 times the frames), export at 30 with `fps_base` 1, and fail a file whose rate is not 30 (`adapter/anim_frame_range.py`: `retime_action`, `verify_fps`). `live_helpers.setup_scene` starts a new scene at 30. `check_unit_files.py` fails a soldier's, rider's or creature's clip that is not at 30 (UNIT-ANIM-FPS, an error now); vehicle, plane and ship types keep the 10 to 60 warning.
- Give every 3D unit its own animations before any clip is paid for (decision 3D-6: an identical set only for reskins of one base unit). New `plan_clips.py --jobs-config <registry>` gives each role of each job the first candidate clip of its unit type that no other unit plays yet, gives a job with `reskin_of` exactly its base's clips, keeps a job's own actions, and escalates naming the unit and role when every candidate is taken. `--write` stores the plan as each job's `required_actions`, which `run_pilot.py` already uses as they are. A registry with `"animation_policy": "authored"` plans no library clip and lists every role for live authoring. The skill's clip rule names the reskin exception.
- Measure only a generated body's large parts, and catch floating debris before the paid rig. A smart-topology generation keeps an armoured figure's plates, a quiver and a held bow as separate parts (an elven archer welds to 96), and a bow in hand reaches below the knee: `measure_body.py` now measures the parts with at least 15 % of the largest part's surface and fails a model with pieces not linked to the body through parts within vanilla's 2 % loose-part distance (BODY-DEBRIS). On the real archer, spearman, both yetis and the warhorse every body keeps its preset and none has debris. A seated rider's idle, move, retreat and training may be one clip (the mount's gait carries it), so UNIT-ANIM-SAME no longer fails them. A regression test puts ninety skinned plates on a unit for UNIT-LOOSE-PART.
- Refitting a weapon replaces it. Running `fit_prop.py` again on a unit it had already armed added a second weapon, and the first one's hand-bound vertices moved the palm it measures; the second round of the 3D-4 acceptance run found it. The earlier prop shape is now removed first and the report names it. The guide gives the order (mesh repairs, then the weapon) and the values to try when a weapon clips.
- Stop padded and cut 3D clips. io_pdx_mesh's animation export operator ignores its start and end frames unless the scene's custom-range setting is on, and exports the scene's frame range: clips authored live came out padded or cut to 80 or 85 samples. The route's adapter already exported each action with its own range; it now also fails when an exported `.anim`'s sample count differs from its action's frame count (`adapter/anim_frame_range.py`). The live helpers gain `export_clip`, which exports an action with its own range, restores the scene's range and runs the same check, and the live authoring guide says never to use the operator.
- Close the gaps a mid-tier acceptance run found in the 3D route. `fit_prop.py` passes `--unit-type` on to the check it names and writes the weapon's `meshsettings` block to `registration_prop.md`. A passing `check_unit_files.py --verdict` now offers the free `repair_unit.py --repairs holes` first when the unit has small open loops, which stay within vanilla's limits but show as holes to a reviewer. The modder guide says which copy of the unit to review after the weapon and repair steps, and maps each kind of reviewer finding to its fix: free repairs for holes, torn joins and grounding, another grip for a weapon through the body, and Blender for clips that twist the body or do not read as their role.
- Ask for closed fists in the reference image of any unit that holds a weapon or tool. Meshy's humanoid rig has no finger bones, so an open hand can never be posed into a grip: one Chaos Redux unit failed seven fresh reviews on its open palm through finger posing, hull shells and modelled fist blocks. The weapon itself stays a separate model, seated in the fist by `fit_prop.py`. The live authoring guide gains what the authors learned since: key before rendering, a grip shell for a hand that is already open, a dark core behind prop vents, checking joints and empty chain bones on extra-limb rigs, counting limbs from the geometry, whole-body changes that make each role read at map zoom, and re-checking earlier review rounds' findings.
- Rewrite the 3D guide's walk-through as one command per step with its verdict: a candidate run, the free body check before any rig is paid for, the full run, the weapon in hand, the checks, the free repair and the independent review.
- Add `fit_prop.py`, the 3D route's free step that puts a held weapon in a unit's hand (decision 3D-4's prop binding), ported from the ZIN 3D session's script after a fixed grip drove the yeti's club into the ground. It closes the provider prop (the tallest piece of a sheet, welded and capped), scales it to a share of the unit's height, puts its grip in the palm of the named hand bone, scores 42 directions over every clip by how often its far end passes through the deformed body or below the ground, binds the best to the hand as its own shape and exports the unit again. The prop's maps are copied and packed like a unit's, so provider downloads stay untouched. The verdict passes when the prop stays out of the ground and inside the body in at most 2 % of its sampled points, and otherwise escalates to another grip or a hand-made hold. On the shipped ZIN yeti with its A/B club, the best grip still clipped 2.9 % of points and escalated.
- Judge a 3D unit's separate parts by whether they stay attached, not by how many there are. The candidate gate no longer fails a model for its count of loose parts: a smart-topology generation keeps an armoured figure's plates, straps and gear as separate closed parts by design (an elven archer arrived in 96), and the count stays in the record. After export, `check_unit_files.py` now fails a loose part with unskinned vertices, or one that sits or drifts farther from the rest of the unit, at rest or in any frame of any clip, than any loose part of vanilla `GER_infantry` or `cavalry_horse` over every clip they play (UNIT-LOOSE-PART, 2 % of the unit's height). Selection boxes (`Collision` shapes) are skipped, which `lib/pdx_deform.py` now marks when it loads a mesh. The verdict points to the job's `loose_part_edits` bind rule, which fixes it for free.
- Pick a body preset by measuring the generated model, before any rig is paid for (decision 3D-4). New `measure_body.py <job>` reads the provider GLB (or an exported `.mesh`), counts the feet on the ground and measures torso width, lower-body reach and the height of the surface's centre (`lib/body_measure.py`, numpy only), and scores the presets in new `config/body_presets.json`: humanoid, large biped and quadruped, each with measured ranges and the anatomy it allows. Each unit type in `unit_types.json` names its `body`. The verdict passes when the job's unit type fits the measured body, fails with the unit types that do, and escalates to live authoring when no preset fits (wings, extra limbs, tentacles, a vehicle, a rider fused to its mount). A job declares its anatomy from the reference image; without it the confidence is capped and a weak fit fails. `body_preset` with a `body_preset_reason` overrides the choice. On 7 Meshy generations, 15 exported ZIN units, 37 Chaos Redux units and 5 vanilla units every body landed on its preset, and a horse given an archer job failed with `mount` and `quadruped_beast` as the fitting types.
- Give each step of the 3D route a short verdict (decision 3D-4). `check_unit_files.py --verdict <file>`, `repair_unit.py` and `review_unit.py` write and print one JSON line (`lib/stage_verdict.py`): `pass` with the next stage's command, `fail` with the exact repair command (all needed repairs in one call, in the right order), or `escalate` with the rules no script fixes and where to read about live authoring. Stretch that stays after the free repair escalates. `review_unit.py` adds a diagnostics sheet per clip at its worst-stretch, lowest and highest frames, colouring the surface by what the checks measure: yellow to red for stretched or squashed edges, cyan for open edges, blue below the ground, with back faces still culled on magenta. The pipeline now stops with a clear message under Python older than 3.10 or without numpy, instead of failing inside a tool.
- Add the escalation path's helpers for authoring 3D unit animations live in Blender (`.tools/3d_pipeline/authoring/live_helpers.py`) and the skill's guide `references/live-authoring.md`. They come from the Chaos Redux and ZIN authors' own helpers: a temporary IK that copies the pose and removes the constraint, a two-bone leg solve with the knee toward the toes, per-key planting of a foot's lowest skinned vertex, a lean spread down the spine, crotch seam weighting across the pelvis and both thighs, grips recorded in the prop's own frame and seated at every key, knuckle-line and contact measures for held props, and review shots with an optional flat-colour pass that makes hand contact readable on dark units. Only `key` inserts keyframes and no helper keys inside a loop over frames, which a test enforces, so authoring stays live (decisions 3D-3 and 3D-5). A Blender test poses, plants, weights and seats a grip on a small synthetic unit.
- Check 3D units for the motion defects found by hand on Chaos Redux and ZIN. `check_unit_files.py` now fails a knee that bends backward against the way the pelvis faces (UNIT-ANIM-KNEE: a leg solver picked the wrong side in five of the portal raider's clips, and the shipped gorilla and oracle attacks bend 39 % and 37 % of leg length backward), a crotch whose vertices follow one thigh each with no blend (UNIT-SEAM: the portal raider's tore to 20.8 % of its height in idle), a clip that barely moves (UNIT-ANIM-STILL), and two roles of one unit that play the same motion (UNIT-ANIM-SAME: the cave monster's move and retreat). With `--mod-root` or `--compare-units` it also fails a clip that another unit of the mod plays (UNIT-ANIM-SHARED), unless the unit is a declared reskin (`--reskin-of`). It compares how far each joint turns over time, which does not depend on the rig's rest pose: one library clip on two units measures under 1 degree, different clips 1.4 degrees and more. Legs are found from the rest pose by height and parentage, not bone names. The seam, knee and stillness limits are measured on vanilla units by `calibrate_deformation.py`, which kept every earlier limit unchanged.
- Add four skills for systems that had none (decision R-10): `hoi4-localisation`, `hoi4-on-actions`, `hoi4-ideologies` and `hoi4-scripted-triggers-effects`. Each is a checklist of at most 12.2 KB whose claims carry an evidence tag and were counted or read in the installed game (1.19.4.2, the build installed on the maintainer's machine, since 1.19.4.1 is not), with links to the wiki by heading. Open points that the files could not settle are listed at the end of each skill. `hoi4-events` and `hoi4-characters-leaders` point to them.
- Fix holes in 3D units made by the route itself. The first Blender step recalculated face winding on the provider mesh before any weld. Meshy's mesh is split at every UV seam, so each UV island was oriented on its own and thin fur, cloth and leaf cards came out facing inward: the ZIN yeti's own rigged mesh went from 11 flipped edges and 0.02 % of its silhouette missing to 22,486 and 7.1 %, and the later weld's rollback kept those normals. `triangulate_and_normals` now recalculates on a welded copy and flips only the split faces whose welded twin disagrees (`wind_like_welded_copy`). Found and measured by the ZIN 3D session.
- Hold the instruction template and every skill to a context budget (maintainer decision R-1). `CLAUDE_template.md` went from 59,912 to under 16,000 bytes and no `SKILL.md` is above 20,000 bytes, down from 58,000. The limits are 4,000 tokens for the template, 5,000 for each `SKILL.md` and one sentence of at most 200 characters for each skill description, estimated at 4 bytes per token (`.tools/sync/read_limits.json`). `check_guidance_size.py` fails above them in the source package, which the Validate workflow already runs through `generators.py --check` and `check.py full`. An installed mod keeps only the single-read ceiling.
- Move the template's long rules into the new `hoi4-project-rules` skill, one reference per topic, unchanged apart from placeholders, and keep a short summary of each section in the template. Section numbers and the Setup placeholders, component blocks and provider markers stay. Eighteen skills (`hoi4-feature-assets`, `hoi4-events`, `hoi4-decisions-missions`, `hoi4-feature-planning`, `hoi4-focus-trees`, `hoi4-subagents`, `hoi4-map-states`, `hoi4-diplomacy-war`, `hoi4-units-divisions`, `hoi4-super-events`, `hoi4-characters-leaders`, `hoi4-ideas-modifiers`, `hoi4-ai-strategies`, `hoi4-improvement-loop`, `hoi4-frame-animation`, `hoi4-technology-doctrines`, `hoi4-debug-playtest`, `hoi4-equipment`) became routers with the always-apply rules, a reference map and their detail in `references/`. Every section moved word for word, and each router names the reference to read for each task. Agent prompts that read these skills say that a `SKILL.md` is a router.
- Shorten every skill description to one sentence. A description with a colon followed by a space is now rejected unless it is quoted, because plain YAML cannot hold it and a runtime may skip the skill.
- Add six workflow lessons to the skills that own them (L-012 to L-017 in `LESSONS.md`). An interface pass starts from an assembled-screen mockup, and every capture is read against a defect checklist before it is reported. Each icon socket gets its own art, and a duplicate check is never re-baselined. Sapient armed fighters keep their weapon in hand and no two units share a library clip. Sessions in a shared checkout revert only their own hunks. A hand-drawn reference map is followed for placement and shapes, and its omissions are filled by judgment and recorded.
- Make `hoi4_wiki/` lean for agents. A page is now its title and its modding text only: the capture line, table of contents, navigation footer, review blocks, `<!-- kit:... -->` markers, heading anchors, evidence tags and "Kit note" labels are gone, and the kit's corrections read as plain text in place. The 64 pages shrink from 4.29 to 3.61 million characters (16 percent, about 171,000 tokens at four characters a token). On the 163 hand-checked questions of HOI4 Agent Tools' discovery benchmark, search answers stay as good (131 rather than 130 answers within five results, 108 landed within three either way, and 93 rather than 95 in first place after small reorderings), no excerpt carries maintenance text any more (30 of 512 wiki excerpts did), and the median reply is 9 percent smaller. Attribution for the CC BY-SA 3.0 text moves to `hoi4_wiki/SOURCES.json` (every page's wiki page, URL, revision and capture date) and `hoi4_wiki/LICENSE.txt`. The kit's text, its evidence and the page reviews move to sidecars in `.tools/wiki/kit/`, which mods do not receive, and `.tools/wiki/manifest.json` records where each kit block sits and the hash of each page's wiki text. The new `.tools/wiki/render_wiki.py` rebuilds pages from the sidecars. The sync writes lean pages on a re-import, `wiki_status.py` keeps reviews in the sidecars, and the verify check fails on any maintenance text in a page, on a page that does not match its sidecar and on evidence whose removal would break a sentence. Section links use GitHub-style heading ids.
- Add `repair_unit.py`, a free Blender repair for an exported unit's mesh: `winding` makes every surface face one way, `bridges` cuts surfaces that join body parts that move apart (an arm fused to the hips) and closes both sides, `holes` closes small open loops, `weights` blends existing weights across a seam inside one skin and limits influences to four, and `ground` shifts a clip's root translation keys so the feet meet the ground in every frame (new `lib/pdx_ground.py`, no Blender needed). It keeps the skeleton, materials, the cut-free vertices' weights and the imported normals, so the existing `.anim` files still play. It writes a repaired copy and reports the checks before and after. On a Chaos Redux creature it removed 157 hip-to-arm faces and 9 flipped faces, and the unit then passed every check.
- Make an independent visual review a required step of the 3D route. New `review_unit.py` imports a unit's exported `.mesh` and `.anim` files into Blender and renders every clip from front, left, back, right, the HOI4 map camera and below, at evenly spaced frames plus each clip's worst-stretch, lowest and highest frames. Back faces are culled as in the game and the background is magenta, so holes show. It writes one contact sheet per clip and `REVIEW_REQUEST.md` with the reviewer prompt (`config/visual_review_prompt.md`), and `--record` saves a fresh reviewer's reply as `verdict.json`. A unit passes only on `VERDICT: pass` with no listed defect. The skill, the production guide and `docs/3d-units.md` say a unit is finished only after that review.
- Check 3D units for holes and deformation from the exported bytes. `check_unit_files.py` now welds the mesh and measures open edges and loops, triangles facing the wrong way, inside-out parts, and surfaces joining body parts that move apart (a hand fused to a thigh), then skins every frame of every `.anim` as the game does and measures stretched and squashed edges and ground contact. New `lib/pdx_deform.py` does the skinning without Blender. The limits are the worst values of the game's own units, measured by the new `calibrate_deformation.py` over 44 soldier, rider and animal meshes (173 clips), 75 land vehicles and 69 planes and ships, and stored in `config/deformation_calibration.json`. Rules: UNIT-HOLE, UNIT-WINDING, UNIT-INSIDE-OUT, UNIT-BRIDGE, UNIT-ANIM-STRETCH, UNIT-ANIM-GROUND. On the Chaos Redux units they find the fused arms of a creature (157 bridge edges against vanilla's 12), flipped triangles and stretched cloth that the earlier checks passed.
- Move the Meshy MCP pin from 0.6.7 to 0.6.9 (runtime lock, bootstrap identities and the tool schema lock re-captured from a live `tools/list`). 0.6.9 adds `texture_model` to `meshy_image_to_3d`, which picks the texture model apart from the geometry (`meshy-7`, `meshy-n1` or `meshy-6-lite`). It needs HOI4 Mod Setup with the 0.6.9 launcher (klimPaskov/HOI4-Mod-Setup#165), and projects on the 0.6.7 runtime need **Repair**. The profiles keep `meshy-7`.
- A mount or four-legged beast generation no longer sends `pose_mode`. Meshy's a-pose and t-pose are for humanoids only.
- `docs/3d-units.md` gives a mount's cost as 35 credits (model 30, remesh 5) and a mounted unit's as about 84.
