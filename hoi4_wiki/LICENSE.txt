Offline Hearts of Iron IV Wiki snapshot: source, license and changes
=====================================================================

Source
------
The pages in this folder are adapted from the Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki
(hoi4.paradoxwikis.com), written by its editors. SOURCES.json in this folder
lists every page with the wiki page it was adapted from, its URL, the revision it
was captured from and the capture date, or marks it as a kit page.

Changes made to the wiki text
-----------------------------
* Each page was converted from the wiki's rendered HTML to Markdown. Navigation
  chrome, tables of contents, edit links and maintenance banners were removed,
  tables and code samples were reshaped for Markdown, and links were pointed at
  local files or reduced to their text.
* The Agentic HOI4 Modding Kit contributors added text to the wiki pages, in
  place and without markers, to correct, update and extend them against the
  installed game. The kit's source repository
  (github.com/klimPaskov/Agentic-HOI4-Modding) records exactly which passages
  the kit wrote, in .tools/wiki/kit/.
* Pages that SOURCES.json marks as kit pages were written in full by the kit
  contributors. Most cover systems the wiki has no page for; "Defines additions"
  supplements the wiki's Defines page, whose text it leaves unchanged.

------
The files in media/ are the images those wiki pages display. Many show Hearts of
Iron IV game artwork or screenshots. Their copyright belongs to Paradox
Interactive AB or to the uploader named on the wiki, and the wiki uses them under
