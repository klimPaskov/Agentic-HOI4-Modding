Offline Hearts of Iron IV Wiki snapshot: source, license and changes
=====================================================================

Source
------
The pages in this folder are adapted from the Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki
(hoi4.paradoxwikis.com), written by its editors. The first line of every page
names the wiki page and the revision it was captured from, and the capture date.

Changes made to the wiki text
-----------------------------
* Each page was converted from the wiki's rendered HTML to Markdown. Navigation
  chrome, edit links and maintenance banners were removed, tables and code
  samples were reshaped for Markdown, and links were pointed at local files or
  reduced to their text.
* Text between "<!-- kit:begin ... -->" and "<!-- kit:end ... -->" markers was
  written by the Agentic HOI4 Modding Kit contributors. It corrects, updates and
  extends the wiki text against the installed game, and says which build it was
  checked against.
* Pages whose header carries "<!-- kit:page -->" have no wiki counterpart. The
  kit contributors wrote them in full.
