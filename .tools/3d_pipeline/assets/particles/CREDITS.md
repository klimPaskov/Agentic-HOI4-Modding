# Particle sprites

Sprites the effects layer puts on vanilla particle systems ([docs/3d-effects.md](../../../../docs/3d-effects.md)). Each was made by hoi4-imagegen from real references, and its record (`<name>.ledger.json`) lists the references, the settings, the review and the output's SHA-256. EFFECT-SPRITE checks that record.

## feather_1.dds

A single loose white contour feather, 128 by 128 DXT5 with alpha, for `feather_shed`. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0), because one of its references is CC BY-SA 4.0. A mod that ships it carries this credit.
